How we calculate

1

US price + sales tax

US prices are listed before tax. We add the average combined state + local sales tax for the state you pick. Tourists can't claim it back.

2

Convert to rupees

We use the live USD→INR mid-market rate, plus any forex markup your card charges (typically 0–3.5%).

3

Customs (optional)

Bringing it home? Each adult gets a ₹50,000 duty-free allowance. Anything above is charged ~38.5% duty.

4

India price incl. GST

Indian MRPs already include GST (18% for these products). We split out the base price so you can compare like-for-like.

Apple and Samsung prices are read automatically from their official US and India stores (last run: ). Products marked “Manual price” are entered by hand.
