How we calculate

1

Store price + local tax

Prices abroad already include VAT/GST — except the US, where we add the sales tax of the state you pick.

2

Tourist refund (optional)

Dubai, Singapore and Thailand give tourists part of the VAT back at the airport. The UK and US don't.

3

Convert to rupees

Live mid-market exchange rates, plus any forex markup your card charges (typically 0–3.5%).

4

Customs (optional)

Each adult can bring ₹75,000 of goods duty-free (Baggage Rules 2026). Above that, about 38.5% duty. Use the trip basket to add up several items.
